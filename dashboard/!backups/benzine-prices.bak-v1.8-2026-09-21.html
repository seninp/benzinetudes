Source — official French fuel-price open data, donnees.roulez-eco.fr (Ministry of the Economy). Annual archives for 2025 and 2026 plus the instantaneous feed, fetched 2026-09-14.

Method — stations selected by great-circle distance ≤ 15 km from Castanet-Tolosan (43.5161 N, 1.5000 E). Each station's last posted price per day is carried forward until its next update, and stops counting 30 days after its final update, so closed sites drop out. A day counts only where at least 3 stations report. Series start 2025-01-15, after the archives' station coverage stabilises.

Brands — the official feed has no brand field. Brands come from OpenStreetMap fuel features, matched to each station by position within 250 m; 65 of the 71 stations matched. Where two stations sit within ~100 m and OSM maps one feature, the further one is flagged “?”.

Ceilings — two figures exist for every ceiling share and they differ. The instant feed counts only what is reported right now; the annual archive counts any station that reported within 30 days. For diesel they nearly agree (20,0% against 22,0% of stations at €2,250); for petrol they do not (15,7% against 23,5% for E10). The reason is per-fuel reporting churn, measured by scripts/snapshot_churn.py across two consecutive snapshots: 222 stations started reporting E10 overnight and 220 of them at exactly €1,990, against 74 diesel arrivals on a base three times larger. Capped sites drop in and out of the feed and always arrive at the ceiling, so the snapshot figure is a lower bound and the archive is the better estimate of the ceiling's reach. The ceiling-share history is still drawn for diesel only, where the churn is small enough to ignore.
