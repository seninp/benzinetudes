Source — official French fuel-price open data, donnees.roulez-eco.fr (Ministry of the Economy). Annual archives for 2025 and 2026 plus the instantaneous feed, fetched .

Method — stations selected by great-circle distance ≤ 15 km from Castanet-Tolosan (43.5161 N, 1.5000 E). Each station's last posted price per day is carried forward until its next update, and stops counting 30 days after its final update, so closed sites drop out. A day counts only where at least 3 stations report. Series start 2025-01-15: the 2025 archive's coverage ramps from 1 876 stations on 1 January to 8 999 on the 15th, 94% of the year's steady state, and a mean over a fifth of the country's pumps is not the same quantity as a mean over all of them.

Brands — the official feed has no brand field. Brands come from OpenStreetMap fuel features, matched to each station by position within 250 m; 65 of the 71 stations matched. Where two stations sit within ~100 m and OSM maps one feature, the further one is flagged “?”.
